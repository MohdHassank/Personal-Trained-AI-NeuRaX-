🧠 Neurax – Your Personal Voice-Powered AI & Device Control Assistant

**Neurax**is a next-generation personal AI that combines **natural voice interaction**, **intelligent reasoning**, and **device control** in one powerful system. It understands your commands, talks back like a human, and can directly **operate your device to perform any task** — from opening apps and managing files to automating workflows and answering complex questions.

✨ Features
- Voice-Based Interaction – Talk to Neurax naturally, just like speaking to a friend.
- Human-Like Conversations – Context-aware responses for accurate and meaningful dialogue.
- Full Device Control – Launch applications, control settings, manage files, and more.
- Smart Knowledge Engine – Delivers precise answers from vast data sources.
- Data Analysis & Reports – Processes and summarizes information instantly.
- Custom Commands – Add your own personalized voice commands.
- Privacy-First – Keeps your data secure and under your control.

🛠 Tech Stack
- Core: Python / Node.js
- AI Engine: Transformer-based LLM (custom fine-tuned)
- Speech Recognition: Vosk / Whisper / Google Speech API
- Device Control: OS automation APIs & scripts
- APIs: OpenAI / HuggingFace / Custom Integrations
- Database: MongoDB / PostgreSQL for AI memory
- Interface: Voice, CLI, and Web dashboard

 📌 How It Works
1. Speak or Type a Command – Neurax listens and understands your request.
2. Thinks & Plans – Uses AI reasoning and stored preferences to decide the best action.
3. Executes Tasks – Directly performs the command on your device or provides an answer.
4. Learns & Improves – Adapts to your voice, preferences, and workflow over time.
